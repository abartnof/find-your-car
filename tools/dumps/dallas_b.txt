==== SPORTS
78. Who was the first head coach of the Dallas Cowboys?
   A) Don Shula
   B) Tom Landry
   C) Bud Grant
   D) Vince Lombardi
   ANSWER: B) Tom Landry
   DID YOU KNOW? Landry coached the team for 29 seasons, from 1960 through 1988.
79. What was the Cowboys' record in their first season in 1960?
   A) 8-4-0
   B) 0-11-1
   C) 5-7-0
   D) 1-13-0
   ANSWER: B) 0-11-1
   DID YOU KNOW? They became a perennial contender within a few years.
80. The Cowboys got the nickname "America's Team" in which year?
   A) 1995
   B) 1966
   C) 1978
   D) 1960
   ANSWER: C) 1978
   DID YOU KNOW? It came from an NFL Films highlight reel that year.
81. The term "Hail Mary" for a desperate pass was popularized by Roger Staubach's 1975 playoff throw to whom?
   A) Drew Pearson
   B) Michael Irvin
   C) Tony Dorsett
   D) Bob Hayes
   ANSWER: A) Drew Pearson
   DID YOU KNOW? Staubach said he closed his eyes and said a Hail Mary.
82. Which Cowboy holds the NFL career rushing record with 18,355 yards?
   A) Tony Dorsett
   B) Walter Payton
   C) Eric Dickerson
   D) Emmitt Smith
   ANSWER: D) Emmitt Smith
   DID YOU KNOW? Smith broke the record in 2002.
83. How many Super Bowls have the Cowboys won?
   A) 7
   B) 3
   C) 2
   D) 5
   ANSWER: D) 5
   DID YOU KNOW? They won Super Bowls VI, XII, XXVII, XXVIII and XXX.
84. The Cowboys' 1990s dynasty was powered by "The Triplets," Troy Aikman, Emmitt Smith and which receiver?
   A) Dez Bryant
   B) Terrell Owens
   C) Drew Pearson
   D) Michael Irvin
   ANSWER: D) Michael Irvin
   DID YOU KNOW? The trio led Dallas to three titles in four years.
85. Jerry Jones bought the Cowboys in 1989 for about how much?
   A) $20 million
   B) $500 million
   C) $140 million
   D) $1 billion
   ANSWER: C) $140 million
   DID YOU KNOW? He then replaced Landry with Jimmy Johnson.
86. Texas Stadium, the Cowboys' old home, was famous for having what in its roof?
   A) A skylight dome
   B) A giant star
   C) A hole
   D) A retractable panel
   ANSWER: C) A hole
   DID YOU KNOW? Texas Stadium opened in 1971 in Irving.
87. AT&T Stadium, the Cowboys' current home, opened in 2009 in which city?
   A) Dallas
   B) Plano
   C) Irving
   D) Arlington
   ANSWER: D) Arlington
   DID YOU KNOW? It is often nicknamed "Jerry World".
88. The Cowboys' practice facility and headquarters, The Star, is in which city?
   A) Frisco
   B) Plano
   C) Fort Worth
   D) Garland
   ANSWER: A) Frisco
   DID YOU KNOW? It opened in 2016 and includes a hotel and an indoor stadium.
89. The original Dallas Texans of the AFL, owned by Lamar Hunt, later became which team?
   A) Houston Oilers
   B) Kansas City Chiefs
   C) San Diego Chargers
   D) Oakland Raiders
   ANSWER: B) Kansas City Chiefs
   DID YOU KNOW? They moved to Missouri in 1963.
90. Lamar Hunt coined the name "Super Bowl" after seeing his kids play with what toy?
   A) A Super Ball
   B) A pinball machine
   C) A football helmet
   D) A Frisbee
   ANSWER: A) A Super Ball
   DID YOU KNOW? He suggested it jokingly, and it stuck.
91. The Dallas Tornado won which championship in 1971?
   A) North American Soccer League
   B) MLS Cup
   C) NCAA title
   D) U.S. Open Cup
   ANSWER: A) North American Soccer League
   DID YOU KNOW? They were one of the league's early powerhouses.
92. The Dallas Mavericks entered the NBA in which year?
   A) 1995
   B) 1988
   C) 1980
   D) 1970
   ANSWER: C) 1980
   DID YOU KNOW? Their name came from the TV western Maverick.
93. Mark Cuban bought the Mavericks in 2000 for about how much?
   A) $800 million
   B) $50 million
   C) $1 billion
   D) $285 million
   ANSWER: D) $285 million
   DID YOU KNOW? He became one of the NBA's most visible owners.
94. In 2011 the Mavericks won the NBA championship over which team?
   A) Boston Celtics
   B) Chicago Bulls
   C) Los Angeles Lakers
   D) Miami Heat
   ANSWER: D) Miami Heat
   DID YOU KNOW? Dirk Nowitzki was the Finals MVP.
95. Dirk Nowitzki was born in which country?
   A) Sweden
   B) Croatia
   C) Poland
   D) Germany
   ANSWER: D) Germany
   DID YOU KNOW? He spent his entire 21-year career with Dallas.
96. The American Airlines Center, home of the Mavericks and Stars, opened in which year?
   A) 2001
   B) 2009
   C) 1995
   D) 1980
   ANSWER: A) 2001
   DID YOU KNOW? It is located in Victory Park.
97. The Dallas Stars moved to Dallas in 1993 from which city?
   A) Quebec
   B) Hartford
   C) Minneapolis-Bloomington
   D) Winnipeg
   ANSWER: C) Minneapolis-Bloomington
   DID YOU KNOW? They had been the Minnesota North Stars.
98. The Stars won their only Stanley Cup in 1999 against which team?
   A) Detroit Red Wings
   B) Philadelphia Flyers
   C) Buffalo Sabres
   D) Colorado Avalanche
   ANSWER: C) Buffalo Sabres
   DID YOU KNOW? The series ended in controversy over a disputed skate in the crease.
99. Which Stars great scored the most goals ever by a U.S.-born NHL player?
   A) Joe Nieuwendyk
   B) Sergei Zubov
   C) Mike Modano
   D) Brett Hull
   ANSWER: C) Mike Modano
   DID YOU KNOW? His statue stands outside American Airlines Center.
100. The Texas Rangers, who play in Arlington, were formerly which team?
   A) Montreal Expos
   B) Houston Colt .45s
   C) Washington Senators
   D) Kansas City Athletics
   ANSWER: C) Washington Senators
   DID YOU KNOW? They moved to Texas in 1972.
101. The Rangers won their first World Series in 2023 against which team?
   A) Atlanta Braves
   B) Arizona Diamondbacks
   C) Philadelphia Phillies
   D) Houston Astros
   ANSWER: B) Arizona Diamondbacks
   DID YOU KNOW? It was a dramatic run through the playoffs.
102. The Red River Rivalry between Texas and Oklahoma is played each year at which Dallas site?
   A) Texas Stadium
   B) The Cotton Bowl at Fair Park
   C) AT&T Stadium
   D) Reunion Arena
   ANSWER: B) The Cotton Bowl at Fair Park
   DID YOU KNOW? The stadium is split down the middle between burnt orange and crimson fans.
103. In 1987 SMU football received what unprecedented NCAA penalty?
   A) A bowl ban
   B) The death penalty
   C) Probation
   D) A fine
   ANSWER: B) The death penalty
   DID YOU KNOW? The program was shut down for the season for recruiting violations.
104. Which SMU running back won the 1948 Heisman Trophy?
   A) Eric Dickerson
   B) Roger Staubach
   C) Don Meredith
   D) Doak Walker
   ANSWER: D) Doak Walker
   DID YOU KNOW? The Doak Walker Award for top running backs is named for him.
105. The SMU "Pony Express" backfield of the early 1980s featured Eric Dickerson and which other runner?
   A) Herschel Walker
   B) Tony Dorsett
   C) Craig James
   D) Earl Campbell
   ANSWER: C) Craig James
   DID YOU KNOW? Both later played in the NFL.
106. Which golfer grew up in Dallas and won the 1968 and 1971 U.S. Opens?
   A) Arnold Palmer
   B) Byron Nelson
   C) Ben Hogan
   D) Lee Trevino
   ANSWER: D) Lee Trevino
   DID YOU KNOW? Trevino was known for his humor and his unorthodox swing.
107. Jordan Spieth, a Dallas native, won which major in 2015?
   A) The Open Championship
   B) The Masters
   C) The Players
   D) The PGA Championship
   ANSWER: B) The Masters
   DID YOU KNOW? He also won the U.S. Open that year.
108. Dallas native Michael Johnson became the first man to win which Olympic double in 1996?
   A) 100m and 200m
   B) Long jump and 200m
   C) 200m and 400m
   D) 400m and 800m
   ANSWER: C) 200m and 400m
   DID YOU KNOW? He ran a then-world-record 19.32 in the 200 meters.
109. Byron Nelson set the PGA Tour record of how many consecutive victories in 1945?
   A) 7
   B) 20
   C) 15
   D) 11
   ANSWER: D) 11
   DID YOU KNOW? He won 18 times that year, and Dallas's tour stop bears his name.
110. FC Dallas began play in 1996 under what original name?
   A) Dallas Sidekicks
   B) Dallas Toros
   C) Dallas Burn
   D) Dallas Tornado
   ANSWER: C) Dallas Burn
   DID YOU KNOW? The team was renamed in 2005.
111. The Cowboys' defense of the late 1960s and 1970s was nicknamed what?
   A) Purple People Eaters
   B) Doomsday Defense
   C) Steel Curtain
   D) Orange Crush
   ANSWER: B) Doomsday Defense
   DID YOU KNOW? Bob Lilly and Randy White were among its stars.
112. Reunion Arena, former home of the Mavericks and Stars, was demolished in which year?
   A) 2001
   B) 1995
   C) 2015
   D) 2009
   ANSWER: D) 2009
   DID YOU KNOW? It opened in 1980.
113. The Cotton Bowl Classic moved from Dallas to which stadium in 2010?
   A) Rose Bowl
   B) Cowboys Stadium in Arlington
   C) Superdome
   D) Reliant Stadium
   ANSWER: B) Cowboys Stadium in Arlington
   DID YOU KNOW? It had been played at the Cotton Bowl since 1937.
114. The Cotton Bowl hosted matches during which international tournament in 1994?
   A) The Olympic Games
   B) The Pan American Games
   C) The FIFA World Cup
   D) The Copa America
   ANSWER: C) The FIFA World Cup
   DID YOU KNOW? It was the only Texas stadium used.
115. The Cowboys won Super Bowl XXVII over which team?
   A) Miami Dolphins
   B) Buffalo Bills
   C) Denver Broncos
   D) Pittsburgh Steelers
   ANSWER: B) Buffalo Bills
   DID YOU KNOW? Dallas won 52-17.
116. Which Naval Academy Heisman winner quarterbacked the Cowboys to two Super Bowl wins in the 1970s?
   A) Don Meredith
   B) Roger Staubach
   C) Danny White
   D) Craig Morton
   ANSWER: B) Roger Staubach
   DID YOU KNOW? Nicknamed "Captain America," he served in Vietnam before his NFL career.
117. Tony Dorsett set an NFL record with a run of how many yards in 1983?
   A) 85
   B) 91
   C) 100
   D) 99
   ANSWER: D) 99
   DID YOU KNOW? It was against the Vikings, and it is the longest possible run, which can only be tied, never broken.
118. In what year was Texas Stadium imploded?
   A) 2012
   B) 2005
   C) 2010
   D) 2016
   ANSWER: C) 2010
   DID YOU KNOW? The Cowboys had moved to their new stadium in Arlington the year before.
119. The WNBA's Dallas Wings moved to Arlington in 2016 from which city?
   A) Seattle
   B) Tulsa
   C) Minneapolis
   D) Houston
   ANSWER: B) Tulsa
   DID YOU KNOW? They had been the Tulsa Shock.
120. The Mavericks lost the 2006 NBA Finals to the same team they beat in 2011. Which one?
   A) Chicago Bulls
   B) Detroit Pistons
   C) Phoenix Suns
   D) Miami Heat
   ANSWER: D) Miami Heat
   DID YOU KNOW? Dwyane Wade starred for Miami in 2006.
121. In February 2025, the Mavericks traded Luka Doncic to which team?
   A) Miami Heat
   B) Golden State Warriors
   C) Los Angeles Lakers
   D) Boston Celtics
   ANSWER: C) Los Angeles Lakers
   DID YOU KNOW? The trade stunned fans, and Anthony Davis went to Dallas in the deal.
122. In which round of the 2016 NFL Draft did the Cowboys select Dak Prescott?
   A) First
   B) Second
   C) Fourth
   D) Seventh
   ANSWER: C) Fourth
   DID YOU KNOW? He took over when Tony Romo was injured.
123. What was the signature accessory of Tom Landry on the sidelines?
   A) A cowboy hat
   B) A clipboard
   C) A fedora
   D) A headset
   ANSWER: C) A fedora
   DID YOU KNOW? He rarely showed much emotion, and the hat became part of his image.
124. The Cowboys won Super Bowl XXX in 1996 over which team?
   A) Denver Broncos
   B) Pittsburgh Steelers
   C) Green Bay Packers
   D) Buffalo Bills
   ANSWER: B) Pittsburgh Steelers
   DID YOU KNOW? Larry Brown, a Cowboys cornerback, was the game's MVP.
125. Who was the first player drafted in Cowboys history, in 1961?
   A) Roger Staubach
   B) Tony Dorsett
   C) Bob Lilly
   D) Troy Aikman
   ANSWER: C) Bob Lilly
   DID YOU KNOW? He became the team's first Hall of Famer.
126. The Texas Rangers' Globe Life Field, with a retractable roof, opened in which year?
   A) 2010
   B) 2020
   C) 2000
   D) 2024
   ANSWER: B) 2020
   DID YOU KNOW? It replaced Globe Life Park next door in Arlington.
127. In 2024, the Mavericks reached the NBA Finals and lost to which team?
   A) Boston Celtics
   B) Golden State Warriors
   C) Denver Nuggets
   D) Miami Heat
   ANSWER: A) Boston Celtics
   DID YOU KNOW? It was Dallas's first Finals trip since 2011.
