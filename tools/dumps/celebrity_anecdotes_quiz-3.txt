1. In December 2001, actress Winona Ryder was arrested after shoplifting more than $5,500 worth of designer merchandise from which Beverly Hills department store?
   A) Barneys New York
   B) Saks Fifth Avenue
   C) Neiman Marcus
   D) Bloomingdale's
   Answer: B
   Fact: Ryder was convicted in 2002 of grand theft and vandalism for cutting security tags off luxury items at Saks Fifth Avenue on Wilshire Boulevard.

2. In 1994, comedian Bobcat Goldthwait shocked host Jay Leno and the studio audience on "The Tonight Show" by doing what right before leaving the stage?
   A) Smashing an electric guitar over the guest desk
   B) Dousing the guest armchair with lighter fluid and setting it on fire
   C) Handcuffing himself to Leno's microphone stand
   D) Diving headfirst into the studio orchestra's drum kit
   Answer: B
   Fact: Goldthwait soaked the chair and ignited it on the May 1994 broadcast; he was later fined and ordered to film public service announcements about fire safety.

3. In 2007, Nicolas Cage outbid Leonardo DiCaprio at an auction to buy a 67-million-year-old dinosaur skull, only to discover later that it was:
   A) A fiberglass replica made for a natural history museum
   B) Illegally smuggled out of the Gobi Desert in Mongolia
   C) Radioactive due to nearby uranium mining deposits
   D) Part of a catalog stolen from the British Museum
   Answer: B
   Fact: Cage voluntarily agreed to repatriate the Tyrannosaurus bataar skull in 2015 after US Department of Homeland Security investigators confirmed it had been stolen from Mongolia.

4. Elvis Presley was infamous for impulsively destroying appliances at Graceland. What did he notoriously do whenever entertainer Robert Goulet appeared on television?

   D) Smashed the picture tube with a hollow-body guitar
   Answer: B
   Fact: Elvis maintained a deep, unexplained distaste for Robert Goulet and shot multiple television sets across his home whenever Goulet’s face flashed on the screen.

   Fact: Officers dispatched to McConaughey's house found him dancing in the buff and banging on bongos with another man present; charges were eventually reduced to a noise fine.
